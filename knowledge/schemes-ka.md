# Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-Dhan Yojana (PM-SYM)

Who can get it:
Any Indian citizen aged 18 to 40 years who is an unorganised worker, including construction site workers. The worker's monthly income must be below Rs 15,000. The worker must not be a member of EPFO, ESIC, or government NPS.

What the worker gets:
This is a voluntary and contributory pension scheme. The worker pays a monthly contribution between Rs 55 and Rs 200 depending on entry age. The Central Government pays an equal matching contribution. After reaching 60 years of age, the worker receives an assured monthly pension of Rs 3,000. If the worker passes away, the spouse gets a 50 percent monthly pension. If husband and wife both join, they receive Rs 6,000 monthly pension jointly.

Where and how to apply:
Apply online at the official portal maandhan.in or visit the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC). You need your Aadhaar card and a savings bank account with IFSC code.

# Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)

Who can get it:
Any Indian citizen aged 18 to 70 years with a savings bank account or Jan Dhan bank account. The worker must give consent for auto-debit of the annual premium.

What the worker gets:
Accident insurance coverage. The scheme provides Rs 2 lakh for accidental death and full disability. It provides Rs 1 lakh for partial disability. The annual premium is Rs 20 per year.

Where and how to apply:
Apply through your bank branch where you hold your savings account, or through your bank's net banking portal.

# Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)

Who can get it:
Any Indian citizen aged 18 to 50 years with a savings bank account or Jan Dhan bank account. The worker must give consent for auto-debit of the annual premium.

What the worker gets:
Life insurance coverage. The scheme provides Rs 2 lakh on death due to any cause. The annual premium is Rs 436 per year.

Where and how to apply:
Apply through your bank branch where you have your savings account, or through your bank's net banking portal.

# Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)

Who can get it:
Eligible unorganised and casual labour families identified under government beneficiary criteria, including rural poor and informal worker households.

What the worker gets:
Free health insurance coverage up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospital care. Treatment is cashless at all empaneled public and private hospitals.

Where and how to apply:
Check your name on the beneficiary list and get an Ayushman card made at any empaneled hospital, a Common Service Centre (CSC), or through the national portal.

# Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)

Who can get it:
Indian citizens living in rural areas who do not own a permanent pucca house, including casual labourers and families without a regular source of income.

What the worker gets:
Financial assistance to build a permanent house. The assistance is Rs 1.2 lakh in plain areas and Rs 1.3 lakh in hilly areas.

Where and how to apply:
Apply through your Gram Panchayat or Block Development Office, or check the official rural housing portal pmayg.nic.in.
